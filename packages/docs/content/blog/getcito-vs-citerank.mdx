## What Happened to GetCito on July 10, 2026

GetCito shipped in November 2025 as its own Next.js application on Firebase with Azure OpenAI behind it. For eight months after that the repository saw only automated dependency bumps and a few README edits.

On July 10, 2026, a pull request titled ["Getcito v9"](https://github.com/ai-search-guru/getcito-worlds-first-open-source-aio-aeo-or-geo-tool/pull/19) landed and replaced the entire codebase with a pnpm and Turborepo monorepo, split into a web app (`apps/web`) and a background worker (`apps/worker`).

## How Actively GetCito Is Maintained

A self-hosted tool is only as current as its repository. GetCito's commit log shows 27 commits from humans across the repository's entire lifetime, and 17 commits in the month after July 10. AI search changes constantly: models get renamed, APIs shift, and new answer surfaces appear every few months. Whoever hosts GetCito takes on the job of keeping pace.

## What CiteRank.com Does

[CiteRank.com](/) is an AI visibility tracker and an AI search agency. The tracker puts your buyer questions to the AI apps you choose (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode) up to four times a day, and keeps every answer in full, so each number traces back to the words an AI wrote. From those answers you see how often you are named for each question and each AI, your share of voice against each competitor, how each answer describes you, which pages each answer cites, and the web searches an AI ran before answering.

About once a week, CiteRank.com ranks what to do next, such as a comparison page your site is missing or a best-of list that names competitors and leaves you out. The tracker never does that work itself. The [AI search agency](/agency) does it every month for businesses that want it done, and each monthly report lists every piece of work, the question it targets, and the AI answers before and after. Tracker plans start at $29/month ([pricing](/pricing)).

## Which Should You Choose?

Choose GetCito if you want open-source code to host yourself and you are prepared to keep it current. Choose CiteRank.com if you want the tracking run for you across ten AI apps, with the option of an agency that acts on what it finds.
