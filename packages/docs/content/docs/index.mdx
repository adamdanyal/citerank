## What Is CiteRank.com?

CiteRank.com is an **AI visibility tracker** and AI search agency. The tracker monitors how AI apps like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews mention your brand in their answers.

For businesses that want the work done, the agency does it every month: it writes the pages your site is missing, gets you onto the review sites and lists AI cites, and reports the AI answers before and after. See [Agency](/agency).

## Key Features

- **AI Visibility Tracking**: Monitor your brand's presence across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode
- **Competitor Intelligence**: Track how competitors appear in AI responses, and your share of voice against each one
- **Sentiment**: See how each answer describes your brand, with the passage behind the score
- **Citation Analysis**: See which sources AI models cite when discussing your brand
- **Query Fan-Out**: See the web searches an AI ran before it answered
- **Prompt Management**: Track the questions your buyers ask, starting from prompts suggested from your website
- **Opportunities**: Get a ranked list of what to do next, refreshed about once a week

## How It Works

CiteRank.com puts your prompts to the AI apps you choose, up to four times a day, and tracks how they mention your brand, competitors, and cited sources over time. It keeps every answer in full, so each number traces back to the words an AI wrote.
