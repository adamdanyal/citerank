## What Is CiteRank.com?

CiteRank.com is an **AI visibility tracker** and AI search agency. The tracker shows how AI apps like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews mention your brand.

For businesses that want help acting on the data, the [agency](/agency) makes the changes every month and reports the AI answers before and after.

## Key Features

- **AI Visibility Tracking**: Monitor your brand's presence across [12 AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers)
- **Competitor Intelligence**: Track how competitors appear in AI answers and your share of voice against them
- **Sentiment**: See how each answer describes your brand, with the passage behind the score
- **Citation Analysis**: See the sources AI cites and the web searches it ran before answering
- **Prompt Management**: Track your buyers' questions, starting from prompts suggested from your website
- **Opportunities**: Get a ranked list of what to do next, refreshed about once a week

## How It Works

CiteRank.com puts your prompts to the AI apps you choose, up to four times a day, and tracks how they mention your brand, competitors, and cited sources. It keeps every answer in full, so each number traces back to what an AI wrote.
