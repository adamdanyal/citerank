## What Is CiteRank.com?

CiteRank.com is an **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** platform. It monitors how AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews mention your brand in their responses.

CiteRank.com is also an AI search agency. If you'd rather hand over the work, the [agency](/agency) makes the changes every month and reports the AI answers before and after.

## Key Features

- **AI Visibility Tracking**: Monitor your brand's presence across ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews, and [ten other AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers)
- **Citation Analysis**: See which sources AI models cite when discussing your brand
- **Competitor Intelligence**: Track how competitors appear in AI responses
- **Prompt Management**: Configure and track prompts relevant to your brand with an AI-powered wizard
- **Automated Reports**: Get a weekly report on what to do next to earn more AI citations
- **Sentiment Analysis**: See how AI answers describe your brand when they mention it

## How It Works

CiteRank.com runs in the cloud, so there is nothing to install. Once you add a brand, it periodically queries AI models with your configured prompts and tracks how they mention your brand, competitors, and cited sources over time.
