## Opportunities Report

About once a week, CiteRank.com reads your tracked answers and citations and ranks what to do next to get cited by AI more often. Open **Opportunities** in the sidebar to see the latest report. The date it was last evaluated is shown at the top.

The report starts with a short **Summary**, then groups its suggestions:

| Group | What It Suggests |
|-------|------------------|
| **Creation** | New content to publish or earn, such as comparisons, guides, and best-of angles for topics where you're absent |
| **Existing Content** | Pages already getting cited that are slipping, or could win the mention with a refresh |
| **Outreach** | Placements on the third-party review sites and editorial roundups AI answers cite |
| **Social** | Community conversations on Reddit, YouTube, and forums that AI answers pull from |

Each suggestion explains why it matters, with three tabs of evidence: the **Prompts** it targets, **Your Citations** for those prompts, and **Competitor Citations**. A **Reality Check** section at the end lists the risks to weigh.

The suggestions are generated by AI from your tracked data, so treat them as a starting point and apply your own judgment before acting. A new brand needs a few days of tracking data before its first report appears.

The tracker suggests the work and leaves it to you. If you'd like it done for you, CiteRank.com's AI search agency does this work every month. See [Agency](/agency).

## Export Charts

Every prompt chart on the **Visibility** page has an **Export (PNG)** button that downloads the chart as an image, ready for a slide or a client report.

## Pull Numbers Into Your Own Reports

The [REST API](/docs/api) serves the analytics behind the dashboard, so you can pull visibility, share of voice, citations, and opportunities into a spreadsheet or BI tool. To ask an AI assistant about your numbers instead, connect it over [MCP](/docs/api/mcp).
