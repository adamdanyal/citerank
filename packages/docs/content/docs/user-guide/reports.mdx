## Opportunities Report

Open **Opportunities** in the sidebar for a ranked list of what to do next to get cited by AI more often. CiteRank.com writes it from your tracked answers and citations and refreshes it weekly. A new brand needs a few days of data first.

## Report Contents

A typical report includes:

- **Summary**: the main findings
- **Creation**: new content to publish for topics where you're absent
- **Existing Content**: cited pages that are slipping or need a refresh
- **Outreach**: placements on review sites and roundups that AI answers cite
- **Social**: Reddit, YouTube, and forum threads AI answers pull from
- **Reality Check**: risks to weigh before acting

Each suggestion explains why it matters and lists its prompts, your citations, and competitor citations. The suggestions are written by AI, so apply your own judgment. To have the work done for you, see [Agency](/agency).

## Sharing Reports

Every prompt chart on the **Visibility** page has an **Export (PNG)** button for slides and client reports. To pull your numbers into other tools, use the [REST API](/docs/api) or [MCP](/docs/api/mcp).
