## Reports

CiteRank.com generates a weekly **Opportunities** report from your tracked answers. It ranks what to do next to earn more AI citations.

## Reading a Report

The report is accessible from the sidebar under **Opportunities**. To read it:

1. Open your brand and click **Opportunities** in the sidebar
2. Read the **Summary** for the main findings
3. Open a suggestion's **Prompts**, **Your Citations**, or **Competitor Citations** tab to see the data behind it

A new brand needs a few days of tracking data first.

## Report Contents

A typical report includes:

- **Creation**: New content to publish for topics where you're absent
- **Existing Content**: Cited pages that are slipping or need a refresh
- **Outreach**: Placements on review sites and roundups that AI answers cite
- **Social**: Reddit, YouTube, and forum threads AI answers pull from
- **Reality Check**: Risks to weigh before acting

To have the work done for you, see [Agency](/agency).

## Sharing Reports

Every prompt chart on the **Visibility** page has an **Export (PNG)** button. To pull your numbers into other tools, use the [REST API](/docs/api) or [MCP](/docs/api/mcp).
