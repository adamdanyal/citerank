This guide walks you through CiteRank.com, from creating your account to monitoring your brand's AI visibility on an ongoing basis.

## Before You Begin

You need a CiteRank.com account with an active plan. To get one, follow the [Quick Start](/docs/getting-started).

## The CiteRank.com Workflow

Here's the typical journey for a new user:

1. **[Register an account](/docs/user-guide/registration)**: create your login
2. **[Set up your brand](/docs/user-guide/brand-setup)**: enter your website and choose [AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers)
3. **[Run the prompt wizard](/docs/user-guide/prompt-wizard)**: get prompts and competitors suggested from your website
4. **[View your dashboard](/docs/user-guide/dashboard)**: see your key metrics
5. **[Track visibility](/docs/user-guide/visibility)**: monitor how AI models mention your brand over time
6. **[Read sentiment](/docs/user-guide/sentiment)**: see how AI answers describe your brand
7. **[Analyze citations](/docs/user-guide/citations)**: see which sources AI models cite alongside your brand
8. **[Manage settings](/docs/user-guide/settings)**: adjust your brand, competitors, prompts, AI apps, and team
9. **[Read your reports](/docs/user-guide/reports)**: see what to do next and share results

CiteRank.com checks your prompts in the background, up to four times a day depending on your plan. Over time, your dashboard fills with trend data showing how your AI visibility evolves.
