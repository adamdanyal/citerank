## Overview

After you create a brand, CiteRank.com opens its **Research Brand Data** page and the **Prompt Wizard**. The wizard analyzes your website and suggests the prompts to track. These prompts are the questions CiteRank.com asks AI models to monitor your brand's visibility.

## Analyze Your Website

Click **Analyze Brand**. CiteRank.com analyzes your website using web search, which takes a couple of minutes. Click **Cancel** to stop it.

## Review the Suggestions

When the analysis finishes, the wizard shows its suggestions in three sections.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the suggestions */}

### Brand Details

Confirm your brand name, website URL, additional domains, and aliases. CiteRank.com uses them to recognize your brand in AI answers.

### Competitors

These are the companies you want tracked alongside your own brand in AI responses. Add, remove, or edit competitors as needed.

### Prompts

Each suggested prompt is a question CiteRank.com will ask AI models. Untick any you don't want, edit tags, or add your own at the bottom.

## Start Tracking

Click **Start Tracking**. The button shows how many new prompts you're adding. CiteRank.com marks your brand as onboarded, takes you to the dashboard, and starts the first check right away.

You can always add, edit, or disable prompts later in **Settings → Prompts**. If you passed on suggestions during setup, or want fresh ideas later, click **Suggest Prompts** there. CiteRank.com adds prompts your brand doesn't track yet to the top of your list, marked as suggested. Edit or remove any you don't want, then save to start tracking the rest. Each brand gets 5 suggestion runs per 24 hours.

## Manual Prompt Creation

To add prompts yourself, go to **Settings → Prompts** and click **Add Prompt**, or **Add Multiple** to paste several at once, one per line. Each prompt has:

- **Text**: the question to ask AI models
- **Tags**: optional tags for filtering and organization
- **Enabled**: whether the prompt is tracked
