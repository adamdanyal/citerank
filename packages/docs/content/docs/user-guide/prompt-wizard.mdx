## Overview

After you create a brand, CiteRank.com opens its **Research Brand Data** page with the **Prompt Wizard**, which analyzes your website and suggests relevant prompts for tracking. These prompts are the questions CiteRank.com asks AI models to monitor your brand's visibility.

If you leave before finishing, the brand's **Overview** shows the wizard again.

## Wizard Steps

The wizard walks you through five stages:

### 1. Analyze Your Website

Click **Analyze Brand**. CiteRank.com researches your website using web search, which takes a couple of minutes. Click **Cancel** to stop it, or **Analyze Brand** again if it fails.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the website analysis step */}

### 2. Confirm Brand Details

Check your brand name, website URL, additional domains, and aliases. CiteRank.com uses them to recognize your brand in AI answers, and citations from your additional domains count as your own.

### 3. Review Competitors

CiteRank.com suggests up to 10 competitors. These are the brands you want to track alongside your own in AI responses.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the competitors step */}

Add, remove, or edit competitors as needed. A competitor is saved only if it has a name and at least one domain.

### 4. Choose Prompts

CiteRank.com suggests up to 30 prompts, each with tags. Untick any you don't want, edit tags, or add your own at the bottom.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the prompts step */}

### 5. Start Tracking

Click **Start Tracking**. The button shows how many new prompts you're adding. Your plan sets how many prompts your organization can track across all its brands; if you tick more than you have left, CiteRank.com tells you how many remain.

## After the Wizard

CiteRank.com marks your brand as onboarded, takes you to the dashboard, and starts the first check right away. Your first results usually appear within a few minutes.

You can always add, edit, or disable prompts later in **Settings → Prompts**. If you passed on suggestions during setup, or want fresh ideas later, click **Suggest Prompts** there. CiteRank.com adds up to 5 prompts your brand doesn't track yet to the top of your list, marked as suggested. Edit or remove any you don't want, then save to start tracking the rest. Each brand gets 5 suggestion runs per 24 hours.

## Manual Prompt Creation

To add prompts yourself, go to **Settings → Prompts** and click **Add Prompt**, or **Add Multiple** to paste several at once, one per line. Each prompt has:

- **Text**: the question to ask AI models
- **Tags**: optional tags for filtering and organization
- **Enabled**: whether the prompt is tracked
