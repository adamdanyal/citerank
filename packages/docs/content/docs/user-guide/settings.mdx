## Settings Overview

The **Settings** section is accessible from the sidebar. Each brand has four sub-pages for managing what CiteRank.com tracks: Brand, Competitors, Prompts, and LLMs.

## Brand Settings

Configure your brand identity:

- **Brand Name**: your brand name as tracked in AI responses
- **Brand Slug**: the part of the dashboard address that names this brand
- **Website**: primary website URL
- **Additional Domains**: other domains your brand owns (e.g. blog subdomain, product domains)
- **Brand Aliases**: alternative names or spellings that AI models might use

## Prompt Settings

Manage your tracking prompts:

- **Add prompts**: click **Add Prompt**, paste several with **Add Multiple**, or click **Suggest Prompts** for AI suggestions that skip what you already track
- **Edit prompts**: modify prompt text and tags
- **Enable/Disable**: toggle tracking for one prompt or several at once without deleting them
- **Remove**: stop tracking prompts you no longer need; their history is kept
- **Premium**: on Pro and Business, also track a prompt on premium models ([details](/docs/user-guide/providers#premium-llm-apis))

Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page to apply your edits.

## Organization Settings

Organization admins manage these settings, which apply to every brand:

- **Team**: invite teammates by email as a **Member** or **Admin**
- **Billing**: change the plan, buy extra premium pairings on Pro and Business, and manage payment methods and invoices
- **API Keys**: issue and revoke keys for the [REST API](/docs/api) and [MCP](/docs/api/mcp)
