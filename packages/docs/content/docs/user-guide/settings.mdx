## Settings Overview

The **Settings** section is accessible from the sidebar. It has four sub-pages for managing your CiteRank.com configuration.

## Brand Settings

Configure your brand identity:

- **Name**: Your brand name as tracked in AI responses
- **Website**: Primary website URL
- **Domains**: Additional domains associated with your brand (e.g. blog subdomain, product domains)
- **Aliases**: Alternative names or spellings that AI models might use

## Competitor Settings

- Add competitor brand names and their domains
- View competitor mentions alongside your brand in visibility data
- Track citation overlap between your brand and competitors

## Prompt Settings

Manage your tracking prompts:

- **Add prompts**: Create new prompts manually, paste several at once, or click **Suggest Prompts** for AI suggestions that skip what you already track
- **Edit prompts**: Modify prompt text and tags
- **Enable/Disable**: Toggle tracking for individual prompts without deleting them
- **Remove**: Stop tracking prompts you no longer need
- **Premium**: On Pro and Business, also track a prompt on premium models ([details](/docs/user-guide/providers#premium-llm-apis))

## LLM Settings

Configure which AI models CiteRank.com queries, within your plan's limits ([details](/docs/user-guide/providers#choosing-ai-apps-for-a-brand)):

- **Scraped Engines**: ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, Bing AI Mode, and Gemini
- **LLM APIs**: Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral, Claude, and Kimi

You can enable or disable individual models.

## Organization Settings

Organization admins manage these settings, which apply to every brand:

- **Team**: Invite teammates as a **Member** or **Admin**
- **Billing**: Change plans, buy extra premium pairings on Pro and Business, and manage invoices
- **API Keys**: Issue and revoke keys for the [REST API](/docs/api) and [MCP](/docs/api/mcp)
