## Settings Overview

The **Settings** section is accessible from the sidebar. Each brand has four sub-pages for managing what CiteRank.com tracks: Brand, Competitors, Prompts, and LLMs. Your organization's settings hold the team, billing, and API keys, which apply to every brand.

## Brand Settings

Configure your brand identity:

- **Brand Name**: your brand name as tracked in AI responses
- **Brand Slug**: the part of the dashboard address that names this brand
- **Website**: primary website URL
- **Additional Domains**: other domains your brand owns (e.g. blog subdomain, product domains)
- **Brand Aliases**: alternative names or spellings that AI models might use

## Prompt Settings

Manage your tracking prompts:

- **Add prompts**: create new prompts with **Add Prompt**, paste several at once with **Add Multiple**, or click **Suggest Prompts** for AI suggestions that skip what you already track
- **Edit prompts**: modify prompt text and tags
- **Enable/Disable**: toggle tracking for individual prompts without deleting them, or select several and enable or disable them together
- **Remove**: stop tracking prompts you no longer need; their history is kept
- **Premium**: on Pro and Business, also track a prompt on premium models, each of which uses one of your organization's premium pairings ([details](/docs/user-guide/providers#premium-llm-apis))

Click **Save Changes** in the bar at the bottom of the page to apply your edits. Your plan sets how many prompts your organization can track across all its brands.

## Team

Organization admins invite teammates from **Settings → Team**. Enter an email address, choose **Member** or **Admin**, and click **Invite**. Only admins can choose the plan and issue API keys.

## Billing

**Settings → Billing** shows your plan, what it costs, and how much of it you're using. Organization admins can change the plan there, buy extra premium pairings on Pro and Business, and click **Manage Billing** to manage payment methods and invoices in the Stripe customer portal.

## API Keys

Organization admins issue and revoke keys for the [REST API](/docs/api) and the [MCP server](/docs/api/mcp) from **Settings → API Keys**.
