## Create an Account

Open [app.citerank.com/auth/register](https://app.citerank.com/auth/register).

You'll see the CiteRank.com sign-up page, with a **Continue with Google** button above the form.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the CiteRank.com sign-up page */}

Fill in your details:

- **Name**: Your display name
- **Email**: Your email address
- **Password**: A secure password

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the registration form */}

Click **Create Account**, then click the link in the verification email CiteRank.com sends you. You'll be signed in automatically and asked to [choose a plan](/docs/getting-started#choose-a-plan).

## Sign In

On return visits, go to [app.citerank.com](https://app.citerank.com) and click **Sign In**. Enter your email and password to access the dashboard.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the login form */}

## Sign-In Methods

The registration flow depends on how you sign up:

| Method | Authentication |
|------|---------------|
| **Email** | Email and password, with a verified address, as described above |
| **Google** | Your Google account, with **Continue with Google** instead of the form |
