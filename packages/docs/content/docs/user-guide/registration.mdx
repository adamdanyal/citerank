## Create an Account

Open [app.citerank.com/auth/register](https://app.citerank.com/auth/register). You can click **Continue with Google** to sign up with your Google account, or fill in your details:

- **Name**: your display name
- **Email**: your work or personal email address (disposable email addresses aren't accepted)
- **Password**: at least 8 characters

Click **Create Account**. CiteRank.com emails you a verification link; click it to verify your address and continue. If the email doesn't arrive, click **Resend Verification Email**. The link expires, so verify soon.

CiteRank.com creates an organization for your account, then asks you to [choose a plan](/docs/getting-started#choose-a-plan).

## Sign In

On return visits, go to [app.citerank.com](https://app.citerank.com) and sign in with Google, or with your email and password.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the login form */}

If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page and CiteRank.com emails you a link to reset it.

## Joining a Team

When an organization admin invites you from **Settings → Team**, you get an email with an invitation link. Open it while signed in with the email address that received the invitation, or create an account with that address first, then click **Accept Invitation**.
