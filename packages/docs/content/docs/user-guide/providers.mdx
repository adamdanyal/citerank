CiteRank.com puts your prompts to 12 AI apps: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode. You choose which ones to track for each brand, within your plan's limits. You don't set up any provider accounts or API keys, because CiteRank.com runs every check for you.

The **LLMs** settings page groups the AI apps by how CiteRank.com reaches them, since each group tells you something different about your brand.

## Scraped Engines

CiteRank.com captures each answer inside the AI app's own interface, so you track what a real visitor sees, including the sources the answer cites:

- ChatGPT
- Google AI Mode
- Google AI Overviews, the AI summary at the top of a Google results page
- Copilot
- Perplexity
- Gemini

## LLM APIs [#direct-model-apis]

CiteRank.com calls these models directly through their APIs, without web search, so their answers show what each model learned in training. Use them to see what a model already believes about your brand:

- Claude
- DeepSeek
- Mistral
- Qwen
- Kimi

Answers without web search cite no sources, so these models don't add to the [Citations](/docs/user-guide/citations) page.

## Premium LLM APIs

CiteRank.com calls these models directly with their own web search turned on, so their answers draw on recent pages and cite them:

- **Claude (web)**
- **GPT Search**
- **Grok (web)**

GPT Search is OpenAI's model called through its API with web search on. The ChatGPT app itself is tracked as a scraped engine.

Premium tracking comes with the Pro and Business plans, and you turn it on prompt by prompt. Each prompt you track on a premium model uses one premium pairing from your organization's allowance, and each pairing is checked once a day. Pro includes 20 pairings and Business includes 30. Extra pairings cost $5/mo each, from **Settings → Billing**.

Premium tracking adds to the AI apps your brand already tracks, which keep running on every prompt. To choose premium models for a prompt, open **Settings → Prompts** and use the **Premium** column ([details](/docs/user-guide/settings#prompt-settings)).

## Choosing AI Apps for a Brand

You first choose AI apps when you [create a brand](/docs/user-guide/brand-setup). To change them later:

1. Open the brand and go to **Settings → LLMs**.
2. Tick the AI apps you want under **Scraped Engines** and **LLM APIs**. A counter shows how many of your plan's picks you're using, for example **3 / 4 picks**.
3. Click **Save Changes** in the bar at the bottom of the page.

Changes apply from the next check. A brand needs at least one AI app, because a brand with none isn't tracked.

The **Premium LLM APIs** card on the same page shows how many of your organization's premium pairings are in use. Its buttons take you to **Settings → Prompts** to choose which prompts use them, and to **Settings → Billing** to change how many you have.

## What Each Plan Includes

| Plan | AI Apps per Brand | Checks per Day | Premium Pairings |
|------|-------------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Starter** | ChatGPT only | 1 | None |
| **Basic** | Any 4 from Scraped Engines and LLM APIs | 4 | None |
| **Pro** | Any 4 from Scraped Engines and LLM APIs | 4 | 20 included |
| **Business** | Any 4 from Scraped Engines and LLM APIs | 4 | 30 included |

On Starter there is nothing to choose, so the LLMs page lists ChatGPT without checkboxes, along with the AI apps the other plans add. Click **Compare Plans** there to see the plans. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the full comparison.

## Query Fan-Out

Before answering, some AI apps search the web. CiteRank.com records the searches each answer ran and shows them on the **Query Fan-Out** page. Not every AI app reveals its searches, so the **Query Availability** card on that page shows, for each AI app you track, whether its searches are available.
