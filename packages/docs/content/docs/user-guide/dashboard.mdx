## The Dashboard

The **Overview** is your home base in CiteRank.com. It shows how AI answers talk about your brand and your competitors.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the overview with data */}

## Key Metrics

Four cards show your figures for the selected period and how they changed:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **AI Visibility** | Share of AI answers that mention your brand |
| **Share of Voice** | Your share of all mentions of you and your tracked competitors |
| **Sentiment** | How favorably AI describes you, from 0 to 100 (50 is neutral) |
| **Position** | Your average place among tracked companies an answer names (1 is first) |

Filters above the cards set the period, AI model, and prompt tags.

## Competitive Landscape

This card compares you with your competitors on one metric at a time. A chart tracks you and your top four competitors, and a grid shows every company by AI model.

Below the card, tracking stats show prompts tracked, evaluations, run frequency, and the last update.

## Waiting for Data

Until the first check finishes, the dashboard shows **Waiting for First Evaluation**. Results usually appear within a few minutes of starting tracking; refresh the page to see them.

## Navigation

Use the sidebar to navigate between sections:

- **Overview**: this page
- **Visibility**: per-prompt visibility data ([details](/docs/user-guide/visibility))
- **Share of Voice**: your mentions versus competitors'
- **Sentiment**: how AI answers describe you ([details](/docs/user-guide/sentiment))
- **Responses**: every AI answer in full
- **Query Fan-Out**: web searches AI models ran
- **Citations**: sources AI answers cite ([details](/docs/user-guide/citations))
- **Opportunities**: what to do next ([details](/docs/user-guide/reports))
- **Settings**: brand, competitors, prompts, and AI apps ([details](/docs/user-guide/settings))
