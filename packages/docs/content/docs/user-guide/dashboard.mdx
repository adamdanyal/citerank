## The Dashboard

The **Overview** is your home base in CiteRank.com. It shows how AI answers talk about your brand and your competitors.

## Key Metrics

Four cards at the top show your headline figures. Each counts every prompt's latest answers as of the end of the selected period, and shows the change from the start of the period to its end.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **AI Visibility** | The share of AI answers to your prompts that mention your brand |
| **Share of Voice** | Your brand's share of every mention of you and your tracked competitors |
| **Sentiment** | How favorably AI talks about your brand when it mentions you, from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral ([details](/docs/user-guide/sentiment)) |
| **Position** | Where your brand comes among the tracked companies an answer names, on average; 1 means it's named first |

Use the filters above the cards to change the period, show a single AI model, or narrow the figures to prompts with certain tags.

## Competitive Landscape

The **Competitive Landscape** card compares you with your tracked competitors on one metric at a time. Switch between AI Visibility, Share of Voice, Sentiment, and Position. A chart shows each company's trend over the period, and a grid shows each company's figure on every AI model you track.

## Tracking Stats

Below that card, CiteRank.com shows how much tracking stands behind the numbers:

| Stat | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Prompts Tracked** | Total number of active prompts being monitored, and the AI models each one runs on |
| **Evaluations** | Total number of times your prompts were checked against AI models in the selected period |
| **Run Frequency** | How often CiteRank.com checks your prompts on each AI model |
| **Last Updated** | When the most recent check ran |

## Waiting for Data

Before the first check finishes, the dashboard shows a **Waiting for First Evaluation** state. CiteRank.com starts that check as soon as you start tracking, and your first results usually appear within a few minutes. Refresh the page to see them.

## Navigation

Use the sidebar to navigate between sections:

- **Overview**: the dashboard described on this page
- **Visibility**: detailed per-prompt visibility data ([details](/docs/user-guide/visibility))
- **Share of Voice**: how often AI models mention you versus your competitors
- **Sentiment**: how AI answers describe your brand ([details](/docs/user-guide/sentiment))
- **Responses**: every AI answer to your prompts, in full
- **Query Fan-Out**: the web searches AI models ran while answering your prompts
- **Citations**: the sources AI answers cite ([details](/docs/user-guide/citations))
- **Opportunities**: what to do next to get cited more often ([details](/docs/user-guide/reports))
- **Settings**: brand, competitors, prompts, and AI apps ([details](/docs/user-guide/settings))
