## The Dashboard

The dashboard is your home base in CiteRank.com. It gives you a high-level view of your brand's AI visibility.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the main dashboard with data */}

## Key Metrics

The dashboard displays four key metrics at the top:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **AI Visibility** | Percentage indicating how often your brand appears in AI responses |
| **Share of Voice** | Your share of every mention of you and your tracked competitors |
| **Sentiment** | How favorably AI describes your brand, from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral |
| **Position** | Where your brand comes among the tracked companies an answer names (1 is first) |

Below them, tracking stats show prompts tracked, evaluations, run frequency, and when the most recent evaluation completed.

## Visibility Trends

A chart shows your visibility score over the selected period. This helps you spot whether your AI visibility is improving, declining, or stable.

## Waiting for Data

If CiteRank.com hasn't run your prompts yet, the dashboard shows a "Waiting for First Evaluation" state. CiteRank.com queries AI models on your plan's schedule. Your first results should appear within a few minutes of starting tracking.

## Navigation

Use the sidebar to navigate between sections:

- **Overview**: The dashboard described on this page
- **Visibility**: Detailed per-prompt visibility data
- **Sentiment**: How AI answers describe your brand ([details](/docs/user-guide/sentiment))
- **Citations**: Citation breakdown by category ([details](/docs/user-guide/citations))
- **Opportunities**: What to do next ([details](/docs/user-guide/reports))
- **Settings**: Brand, competitors, prompts, and LLM configuration
