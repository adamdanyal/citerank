## Your First Brand

After you choose a plan, click **Create Your First Brand**.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the new brand form */}

Enter your **Brand Name** and **Website** (e.g. `https://yourbrand.com`), click **Continue**, pick the [AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers) to track, and click **Create Brand**. CiteRank.com creates your brand profile and redirects you to the dashboard.

## What CiteRank.com Extracts

From your website URL, CiteRank.com identifies:

- **Brand name**: Read from your site and web search
- **Primary domain**: The domain you entered
- **Industry context**: Used to generate relevant prompts in the next step

## Managing Brand Details Later

After initial setup, you can update your brand's details in **Settings → Brand**:

- **Name**: Your brand name as it should appear in AI responses
- **Website**: Your primary website URL
- **Domains**: Additional domains associated with your brand
- **Aliases**: Alternative names or spellings AI models might use
