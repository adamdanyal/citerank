## Your First Brand

Once your plan is active, click **Create Your First Brand** on the **Organizations and Brands** page.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the new brand form */}

Enter your **Brand Name** and **Website** (e.g. `example.com`) and click **Continue**. Then choose which [AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers) to track and click **Create Brand**. CiteRank.com opens the [prompt wizard](/docs/user-guide/prompt-wizard).

## What CiteRank.com Extracts

From your website, the prompt wizard suggests aliases, additional domains, competitors, and prompts to review before tracking starts.

## Managing Brand Details Later

After initial setup, you can update your brand's details in **Settings → Brand**:

- **Brand Name**: your brand name as it should appear in AI responses
- **Brand Slug**: the brand's name in the dashboard address
- **Website**: your primary website URL
- **Additional Domains**: other domains your brand owns
- **Brand Aliases**: alternative names or spellings AI models might use

## Multiple Brands

Starter and Basic include one brand, Pro two, and Business five. Click **New Brand** on the **Organizations and Brands** page to add one, or email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com) for more.
