## Your First Brand

Once your plan is active, the **Organizations and Brands** page lists your organization. Click **Create Your First Brand**.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the new brand form */}

Enter your **Brand Name** and **Website** (for example `example.com`) and click **Continue**.

## Choose AI Apps

Next, choose which AI apps to track for this brand. CiteRank.com selects your plan's default picks for you, so change them only if you want to, then click **Create Brand**. See [AI Apps](/docs/user-guide/providers) for what each one tells you.

CiteRank.com creates the brand and opens it, ready for the [prompt wizard](/docs/user-guide/prompt-wizard).

## What CiteRank.com Extracts

In the prompt wizard, CiteRank.com analyzes your website and suggests:

- **Brand name**: your brand name as it should appear in AI responses
- **Additional domains**: other domains your brand owns
- **Aliases**: alternative names or spellings AI models might use
- **Competitors and prompts**: used to track your visibility from the start

You can edit all of these before you start tracking.

## Managing Brand Details Later

After initial setup, you can update your brand's details in **Settings → Brand**:

- **Brand Name**: your brand name as it should appear in AI responses
- **Brand Slug**: the part of the dashboard address that names this brand
- **Website**: your primary website URL
- **Additional Domains**: other domains your brand owns (for example `blog.example.com`); citations from these domains count as your brand's citations
- **Brand Aliases**: alternative names, product lines, or abbreviations AI models might use

## Multiple Brands

An organization can track as many brands as its plan includes: one on Starter and Basic, two on Pro, and five on Business. To add another, click **New Brand** under your organization on the **Organizations and Brands** page. For more brands than the listed plans include, email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com).
