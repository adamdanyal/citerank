title: Quick Start
description: Sign up for CiteRank.com, choose a plan, add your brand, and read your first AI visibility results.
---

CiteRank.com runs in your browser at [app.citerank.com](https://app.citerank.com), so there is nothing to install. Getting from sign-up to your first results takes five steps.

## Create Your Account

Go to [app.citerank.com/auth/register](https://app.citerank.com/auth/register) and sign up with **Continue with Google**, or with your name, email, and a password. If you sign up by email, CiteRank.com sends you a verification link. Click it to continue.

CiteRank.com creates an organization for you at the same time. An organization holds your brands, your team, and your plan. See [Registration & Login](/docs/user-guide/registration) for details.

## Choose a Plan

Next, CiteRank.com asks you to choose a plan:

| Plan | Price | Brands and Prompts | AI Apps per Brand | Premium Pairings |
|------|-------|--------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Starter** | $29/mo | 1 brand, 50 prompts | ChatGPT, checked once a day | None |
| **Basic** | $99/mo | 1 brand, 50 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day | None |
| **Pro** | $299/mo | 2 brands, 150 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day | 20 |
| **Business** | $649/mo | 5 brands, 350 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day | 30 |

Prompts and premium pairings are shared across the organization's brands. A premium pairing tracks one prompt on one AI model that searches the web itself, once a day ([details](/docs/user-guide/providers#premium-llm-apis)). Annual billing saves two months.

Click **Subscribe** on the plan you want and pay through Stripe Checkout. CiteRank.com activates your organization as soon as the payment goes through. Only an organization admin can choose a plan. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the full comparison.

## Add Your Brand

On the **Organizations and Brands** page, click **Create Your First Brand**. Enter your **Brand Name** and **Website**, then click **Continue**.

Then choose which AI apps to track for this brand. Your plan's default picks are already selected, so change them only if you want to, and click **Create Brand**. You can change them later in **Settings → LLMs**. See [AI Apps](/docs/user-guide/providers) for what each one tells you.

## Review the Suggested Prompts

CiteRank.com opens the brand's **Research Brand Data** page. Click **Analyze Brand**. CiteRank.com analyzes your website using web search and, within a couple of minutes, suggests:

- **Brand details**: your brand name, website, other domains you own, and aliases
- **Competitors**: the companies to track alongside you
- **Prompts**: a starter set of questions your buyers might ask an AI

Untick any prompt you don't want, edit tags, add your own prompts, and adjust the competitors. Then click **Start Tracking**. See [Prompt Wizard](/docs/user-guide/prompt-wizard) for details.

## Read Your Results

CiteRank.com runs the first check as soon as you start tracking. It usually takes a few minutes, and until it finishes the overview shows **Waiting for First Evaluation**. Refresh the page to see your data.

The **Overview** then shows four headline figures for the selected period:

| Figure | What It Measures |
|--------|------------------|
| **AI Visibility** | The share of AI answers to your prompts that mention your brand |
| **Share of Voice** | Your brand's share of every mention of you and your tracked competitors |
| **Sentiment** | How favorably AI talks about your brand when it mentions you, from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral |
| **Position** | Where your brand comes among the tracked companies an answer names, where 1 means named first |

Below them, the **Competitive Landscape** compares you with each competitor on every AI app you track. The sidebar takes you further: **Visibility** for each prompt, **Responses** for every answer in full, **Citations** for the pages AI answers cite, and **Opportunities** for what to do next. Opportunities appear once your prompts have run for a few days.
