---

CiteRank.com runs in your browser at [app.citerank.com](https://app.citerank.com), so there is nothing to install.

## Create Your Account

Go to [app.citerank.com/auth/register](https://app.citerank.com/auth/register) and sign up with **Continue with Google**, or with your name, email, and a password. If you sign up by email, click the verification link CiteRank.com sends you. See [Registration & Login](/docs/user-guide/registration).

## Choose a Plan

CiteRank.com then asks you to choose a plan:

| Plan | Price | Brands and Prompts | AI Apps per Brand |
|------|-------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Starter** | $29/mo | 1 brand, 50 prompts | ChatGPT, checked once a day |
| **Basic** | $99/mo | 1 brand, 50 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day |
| **Pro** | $299/mo | 2 brands, 150 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day |
| **Business** | $649/mo | 5 brands, 350 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day |

[Prompts](/docs/user-guide/settings#prompt-settings) are shared across the organization's brands. Pro includes 20 [premium pairings](/docs/user-guide/providers#premium-llm-apis) and Business 30. Annual billing saves two months.

Click **Subscribe** on a plan and pay through Stripe Checkout. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the full comparison.

## Add Your Brand

On the **Organizations and Brands** page, click [Create Your First Brand](/docs/user-guide/brand-setup), enter your **Brand Name** and **Website**, and click **Continue**. Then choose the [AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers#choosing-ai-apps-for-a-brand) to track and click **Create Brand**. Your plan's default picks are preselected; change them later in [Settings → LLMs](/docs/user-guide/settings#llm-settings).

## Run the Prompt Wizard

On the brand's **Research Brand Data** page, the [prompt wizard](/docs/user-guide/prompt-wizard) walks you through:

1. **Analyze Brand**: CiteRank.com reads your website and searches the web for a couple of minutes
2. **Brand Details**: Confirm your brand name, website, additional domains, and aliases
3. **Competitors**: Add, remove, or edit the [competitors](/docs/user-guide/settings#competitor-settings) CiteRank.com suggests
4. **Prompts**: Untick any suggested prompt you don't want, and add your own
5. **Start Tracking**: Save your choices and start the first check

## Read Your Results

The first check usually takes a few minutes. Until then, the overview shows [Waiting for First Evaluation](/docs/user-guide/dashboard#waiting-for-data). Refresh the page to see your data.

The [Overview](/docs/user-guide/dashboard) then shows four headline figures:

| Figure | What It Measures |
|--------|------------------|
| **AI Visibility** | The share of AI answers to your prompts that mention your brand |
| **Share of Voice** | Your share of every mention of you and your tracked competitors |
| **Sentiment** | How favorably AI describes you, from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral |
| **Position** | Where you come among the tracked companies an answer names, where 1 means first |

The sidebar takes you further: [Responses](/docs/user-guide/sentiment#filtering-responses-by-sentiment) for every answer in full, [Citations](/docs/user-guide/citations) for the pages AI cites, and [Opportunities](/docs/user-guide/reports) for what to do next, once your prompts have run for a few days.

## What's Next

With CiteRank.com tracking, head to the [User Guide](/docs/user-guide) to manage prompts, competitors, and [AI apps](/docs/user-guide/providers), or pull your data into your own tools with the [API](/docs/api).
