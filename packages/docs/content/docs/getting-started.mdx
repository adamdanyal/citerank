CiteRank.com runs in your browser at [app.citerank.com](https://app.citerank.com), so there is nothing to install.

## Create Your Account

Go to [app.citerank.com/auth/register](https://app.citerank.com/auth/register) and sign up with **Continue with Google**, or with your name, email, and a password. If you sign up by email, click the verification link CiteRank.com sends you.

CiteRank.com also creates an organization to hold your brands, team, and plan. See [Registration & Login](/docs/user-guide/registration).

## Choose a Plan

CiteRank.com then asks you to choose a plan:

| Plan | Price | Brands and Prompts | AI Apps per Brand |
|------|-------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Starter** | $29/mo | 1 brand, 50 prompts | ChatGPT, checked once a day |
| **Basic** | $99/mo | 1 brand, 50 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day |
| **Pro** | $299/mo | 2 brands, 150 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day |
| **Business** | $649/mo | 5 brands, 350 prompts | Any 4, checked 4 times a day |

Prompts are shared across the organization's brands. Pro includes 20 [premium pairings](/docs/user-guide/providers#premium-llm-apis) and Business 30. Annual billing saves two months.

Click **Subscribe** on a plan and pay through Stripe Checkout. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the full comparison.

## Add Your Brand

On the **Organizations and Brands** page, click **Create Your First Brand**, enter your **Brand Name** and **Website**, and click **Continue**.

Then choose the AI apps to track. Your plan's default picks are already selected, so click **Create Brand** unless you want others. You can change them later in **Settings → LLMs**.

## Review the Suggested Prompts

On the brand's **Research Brand Data** page, click **Analyze Brand**. CiteRank.com analyzes your website using web search and, within a couple of minutes, suggests your brand details, competitors, and prompts your buyers might ask an AI.

Untick any prompt you don't want, add your own, adjust the competitors, and click **Start Tracking**. See [Prompt Wizard](/docs/user-guide/prompt-wizard).

## Read Your Results

The first check starts right away and usually takes a few minutes. Until it finishes, the overview shows **Waiting for First Evaluation**. Refresh the page to see your data.

The **Overview** then shows four headline figures:

| Figure | What It Measures |
|--------|------------------|
| **AI Visibility** | The share of AI answers to your prompts that mention your brand |
| **Share of Voice** | Your share of every mention of you and your tracked competitors |
| **Sentiment** | How favorably AI describes you, from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral |
| **Position** | Where you come among the tracked companies an answer names, where 1 means first |

The sidebar takes you further: **Responses** for every answer in full, **Citations** for the pages AI cites, and **Opportunities** for what to do next, once your prompts have run for a few days.
