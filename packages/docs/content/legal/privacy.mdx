This policy lays out what we collect and why, how it's handled, and what rights you have over it.

This policy applies to **CiteRank.com** and to the hosted CiteRank.com service, our websites and other online properties, and our APIs (together, the "Services"). CiteRank.com is a trading name of Danx Digital Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 13275434) whose registered office is at 86-90 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NE, United Kingdom ("we", "our", "us"). Danx Digital Limited is the controller of the personal data this policy describes, under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

This policy covers our handling of information about site visitors, prospective customers, and customers and their authorized users. We refer to all of you as "you" throughout.

It does **not** cover information about a customer's own end users that we process on that customer's behalf. For that data the customer is the controller and we are the processor; our obligations are set out in the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and any data processing addendum we've signed with them.

## What we collect and why

### Identity and access

When you sign up we collect what we need to create and run your account — things like your name, email address, and a password, or the profile details your identity provider hands us if you sign in with Google or a similar service. That's so you can have an account, so we can reach you, so your teammates can tell who's who, and so we can run, secure, and improve the Services.

When you sign up, sign in, or reset a password with an email address, a [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/application-services/products/turnstile/) check may run in your browser to keep bots from abusing those forms. Cloudflare receives your IP address and information about your browser to run that check and improve its bot detection, not for advertising.

We use your email address for the service email your account depends on — verification, password resets, teammate invitations, billing and account notices — and, if you opt in, for product news and other marketing. Marketing email carries a way to unsubscribe. Service email doesn't, because your account depends on it.

### Billing information

If you subscribe to a paid plan you'll provide payment and billing details. Card details go to our payment processor rather than to us. We keep the billing records that go with your account — transactions, invoices, and billing details — so we can charge you correctly, calculate tax, support you when something looks wrong, detect fraud, and meet our accounting and legal obligations.

### Product content

We store the content you create in CiteRank.com — the brands you track, the competitors and prompts you configure, the answers CiteRank.com collects from answer engines on your behalf, the reports built from them, and anything else you put into the product. This is what the product is. We generally keep it while the account is open; see [Data retention](#data-retention) for how to have it deleted.

### People named in AI answers

The answers CiteRank.com collects come from public AI answer engines, and they sometimes name people, such as a company's founder or the author of an article an answer cites. When they do, we store that text as part of the answer, along with the sources it cites. We don't look people up, build profiles of them, or use their names for anything other than showing our customers the answers. If you've been named in an answer and want to ask about it, email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com).

### General geolocation data

We log IP addresses when an account is created and when people sign in, and use them for things like security, fraud prevention, abuse mitigation, analytics, and approximate location. We keep them for as long as they're useful for those purposes.

### Website interactions

We may collect information about browsing activity on our websites and in the product for analytics and product development, such as which pages were visited, what referred you, which features got used, and general browser and device information.

Neither our websites nor the product run an analytics tool at the moment. If we add one, we'll list it at [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors), and any tool that stores something on your device will wait for your consent where the law requires it. See [Advertising and cookies](#advertising-and-cookies) below.

### Advertising and cookies

We don't run advertising or conversion-measurement tags on our websites or in the product, and we don't share your information with advertising platforms. We have not sold personal information.

We use first-party cookies to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in and form submissions. Our support chat stores a session identifier in your browser so a conversation survives a page reload.

Because we set no analytics or advertising cookies, we don't show a cookie banner. If we add any, we'll ask for your consent before setting them wherever the law requires it, including in the UK and the EEA, and update this policy first.

The full list of what we set and why is in our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

You can also adjust cookie retention and block individual cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the product will not work if you turn cookies off entirely.

### Support and correspondence

When you email us, or start a chat with us on our website, we keep that correspondence — including your email address — so we have a history to reference if you get in touch again. Chat is provided by a third party listed in our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

We also store information you volunteer, like responses to a survey or a research call. If we want to record a conversation, we'll ask first.

### Error monitoring

When something breaks we collect diagnostic information so we can fix it — the error and the context around it, which typically includes stack traces, request and browser details, IP address, the account involved, and whatever state the error touched. Some of that may go to an error-monitoring provider, and it can include fragments of the content involved.

## Our lawful bases

UK data protection law requires a lawful basis for each way we use personal data. Ours are:

- **Contract.** Creating and running your account, providing the Services you signed up for, billing you, and sending the service email your account depends on.
- **Legitimate interests.** Keeping the Services secure and free of abuse, preventing fraud, fixing errors, improving the product, keeping a record of our correspondence, and storing the AI answers described above. We rely on this only where your interests and rights don't outweigh ours, and you can object at any time.
- **Legal obligation.** Keeping tax and accounting records, and responding to lawful requests from public authorities.
- **Consent.** Product news and other marketing email you've opted in to, and any analytics or advertising cookies we add in future. You can withdraw consent at any time.

## When we access or disclose your information

**To provide the Services.** We use third-party subprocessors for hosting, storage, email, payments, support chat, error monitoring, and the AI and search providers CiteRank.com queries on your behalf. The current list, what each one does, and where it operates, is published at [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors).

**With your direction.** If you connect a third-party service to CiteRank.com, or ask us to send data somewhere, we may do that. That includes AI assistants you connect through our MCP server or API: what they read from your account is then handled under their provider's terms.

**To operate the Services.** We may access your account to provide, support, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.

**To investigate or act on prohibited use.** See our [Acceptable Use policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

**To protect rights and safety.** We may access or disclose information where we believe it's necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of CiteRank.com, our customers, or others.

**Aggregated and de-identified data.** We may aggregate or de-identify information collected through the Services and use it for any purpose, including improving CiteRank.com and publishing industry research. De-identified data does not identify you or your organization.

**When required by law.** We may preserve or disclose information where we believe in good faith that it's required by law, legal process, or a government request, including tax and accounting obligations.

**If we're acquired.** We don't plan on it, but if CiteRank.com is acquired by or merges with another company, your information may be part of what transfers and may become subject to a different privacy policy.

## Your rights over your information

Under the UK GDPR, and other laws depending on where you are, you have some or all of the following rights:

- **Right to know.** What personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold. This policy is that disclosure.
- **Right of access.** To the personal information we hold about you, and to information about how it is shared, stored, secured, and processed.
- **Right to correction.** To have inaccurate personal information corrected.
- **Right to erasure.** To have your personal information erased, subject to limits under applicable law. Fulfilling some deletion requests means we can no longer provide the Services, so a deletion request may result in closing your account.
- **Right to restrict processing** and **right to object**, in the situations where applicable law provides them.
- **Right to portability.** To receive a copy of your data in a portable format. Email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com) to request one.
- **Right not to be subject to automated decision-making** that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.
- **Right to non-discrimination.** We will not charge you differently, or give you worse service, because you exercised a privacy right.
- **Right to complain** to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), or to the supervisory authority where you live. We'd like the chance to put things right first, so please contact us before you complain.

To exercise any of them, email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com), or write to Danx Digital Limited, 86-90 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NE, United Kingdom. We'll respond within one month, as UK law requires. We may need to verify your identity before responding — usually by confirming your name and the email address on the account. If an authorized agent contacts us on your behalf, we'll need written consent from the account holder first.

If we deny a request, we'll explain why in our response.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee the security of information you send us.

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com) rather than disclosing it publicly.

## What happens when you delete something

Content you delete in the product stops being available there. Our backups roll over on a schedule, so a copy can survive in a backup until that backup expires.

**Cancelling a subscription is not a deletion request, and it is not a guarantee of storage.** It stops CiteRank.com collecting anything new and closes off the paid features. It does not on its own erase what is already in the account, and it does not commit us to keeping it: a dormant account may be closed under the [Terms](/legal/terms), and closing an account may delete what is in it. If there's anything you want, ask us for a copy before you cancel.

To actually have it deleted, ask us at [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com). We will delete your account and its contents from our active systems within the time applicable law allows, and remaining copies drop out of our backups as those backups expire. Once that is done we cannot recover any of it, so ask us for a copy first if you want one.

## Data retention

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and then we delete or aggregate it.

Account content has no fixed retention period in either direction. We generally keep it while the account is open, because that is the product; we delete it when you ask us to, and may delete it when an account is closed under the [Terms](/legal/terms). We neither purge it on a schedule nor undertake to preserve it indefinitely.

Billing records are the other exception. We have to keep invoices and transaction records to meet tax and accounting obligations, so those survive an account deletion. UK tax law has us keep them for six years after the end of the financial year they relate to.

We may also keep information longer where we have to: to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements.

We're based in the United Kingdom. Some of the providers behind the Services store or process data in other countries, including the United States and countries in the European Union; see [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) for which. **Assume that information you give us may be transferred to and stored in the United States.**

## Transfers of personal data out of the UK

Personal data that leaves the UK must keep an equivalent level of protection. We rely on the UK's adequacy regulations where they apply, such as for the EEA and for U.S. companies certified under the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Otherwise we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with the provider concerned.

Where we act as a processor for a customer, we will put the transfer terms that customer needs into a data processing addendum. Email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com) to request one.

## Children

The Services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email [team@citerank.com](mailto:team@citerank.com) and we'll delete it.
