This policy explains what **CiteRank.com** stores in your browser when you visit our websites or use CiteRank.com, and what control you have over it. CiteRank.com is a trading name of Danx Digital Limited. This policy supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

"Cookies" here is shorthand. It covers cookies, `localStorage`, and any other technology that stores or reads something on your device.

## The short version

- We store what our websites and the product need to work, and our support chat stores a session identifier so a conversation survives a page reload.
- **We set no analytics or advertising cookies**, and we don't show a cookie banner.
- If we add analytics or advertising cookies, we'll ask for your consent before setting them wherever the law requires it, including in the UK and the EEA, and we'll update this page first.

## Categories

The tables below describe what we set at the time of writing. The categories are the part that matters — what lands in each one changes as the tools we use change, and your choices apply to the category, not to a particular cookie.

### Strictly necessary

These are always set. The site cannot work without them, and consent is not required for them under the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations or the EU ePrivacy Directive.

| What | Where | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `better-auth.session_token` and related session cookies | app.citerank.com | Keeps you signed in | For as long as you stay signed in |
| CSRF and callback state | app.citerank.com | Protects sign-in and form submissions from cross-site request forgery | Minutes |
| Load-balancing and security cookies | all | Set by our hosting providers to route requests and mitigate abuse | Session |
| Cloudflare Turnstile (no cookie) | app.citerank.com sign-up, sign-in, and password reset | Checks that a person, not a bot, is creating an account or asking us to send an email. Turnstile runs in a Cloudflare frame and sets no tracking cookies. | Minutes |
| Sentry session identifier (sessionStorage) | app.citerank.com | Correlates errors from the same visit so a crash report is intelligible. No cookie. | Until the tab closes |

### Analytics

We don't use an analytics tool at the moment. If we add one that stores anything on your device, it will be listed here and will wait for your consent where the law requires it.

### Advertising

We don't run advertising or conversion-measurement tags.

### Support chat

Our support chat, provided by [Crisp](https://crisp.chat/en/privacy/), loads with the page and stores a session identifier, so a conversation survives a page reload.

## Changing your mind

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop you from being able to sign in, and blocking the chat's storage means a conversation won't survive a page reload.

If we add analytics or advertising cookies, a **Cookie preferences** control will appear in the footer of our website and in the legal menu in CiteRank.com, so you can change your answer at any time.
